# Genre glossary

Maps genre nicknames and related words to catalog genres. The last two entries
(K-pop, blues) are genres the glossary knows about but the 18-song catalog does
NOT contain. They exist on purpose, so the agent's critic has to notice the gap
and revise instead of pretending it found a match.
